Squad builder

Pick what each player wants. Every squad gets a win chance from its characters' stats, and its value is win chance × the marks the players can still get.

How the win chance works

Each character has five 0–5 stats:

    For a squad:

    • Kill speed = 0.6 × best Damage + 0.4 × average Damage
    • Staying alive = 0.5 × best Survival + 0.5 × average Survival. Dead players keep fighting as ghosts, so one survivor matters most.
    • Team help = total Team help, each character counted once, max 6. Only counts with 2+ players.
    • Team cost = total Team cost. Only counts with 2+ players.
    • Skill load = how far the average Skill is above 2.5
    • rating = 0.45 × Kill + 0.35 × Alive + 0.3 × Help − 0.2 × Cost − 0.25 × Skill load; win chance = 10% + 17% × rating (5–95%)
    • Goals: Auto counts your marks ×1, Carry ×0.2 (so you get a strong or helpful character), hunting a picked character ×1.5. One run counts at most 5 marks per player.
